## ✨ Key Executive Capabilities

* **📈 100% Reactive Cross-Filtered Overview:** Real-time SVG revenue & YoY trajectory curves, dynamic channel segmentation (`Trade`, `Hospitality`, `Retail`, `Direct`), and interactive date windows (`30 days`, `13 weeks`, `12 months`) that dynamically recalculate all KPI cards, sparklines, and Q4 pace columns.
* **🎛️ What-If Pricing & Elasticity Simulator:** Live sliders for list price shifts (`-15%` to `+30%`), raw material cost inflation (`-20%` to `+40%`), and trade discount tiers with automatic price-elasticity volume modeling and 90-day net profit projection.
* **📄 Interactive CPQ & Quote-to-Order Desk:** Real-time quote discount guardrail simulation and one-click conversion from approved client quotes into active workshop orders.
* **🏢 Multi-Sector Dataset Engine (`⇄`):** Seamlessly switch between **Studio BI**, **Regency Urban Developments (Real Estate & Fit-Out)**, and **AdventureWorks Enterprise** datasets, or upload custom `.CSV` catalogues directly in the browser.
* **💻 6-Language Data Engineering Studio:** Built-in interactive code sandbox featuring production-grade **SQL (Window Functions)**, **Power BI DAX**, **Python (Elasticity Forecasting)**, **C++20**, **Java Enterprise ETL**, and **ES6+ JavaScript** with live dataframe output simulation.
* **⌨️ Spotlight Command Palette (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`):** Instant search across deals, accounts, SKUs, and system actions with 3 architectural ambiance themes (**Warm Architectural**, **Midnight Obsidian**, **Royal Emerald**).
