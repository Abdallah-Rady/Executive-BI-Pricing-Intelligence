## 🌟 Executive Overview & Business Value
**Abdallah Rady by Analysis** bridges the gap between high-level strategic financial planning and day-to-day commercial execution. Designed to replace static reporting with live decision intelligence, the platform allows executives and data analysts to model revenues, inspect pipeline velocity, enforce pricing guardrails, and simulate raw material cost shifts in real-time.

---

## ⚙️ Core Architecture & Features

* **📈 100% Reactive Cross-Filtered Overview:** 
  * Real-time SVG revenue & YoY trajectory curves with custom tooltips and historical comparison bands.
  * Dynamic channel segmentation (`Trade`, `Hospitality`, `Retail`, `Direct`) that instantly cross-filters all KPIs, sparklines, and pace metrics upon click or solo-toggle.
  * Executive Pulse Strip tracking portfolio gross margins, LTV:CAC unit economics, and break-even volume pace.

* **🎛️ What-If Pricing & Raw Material Elasticity Simulator:**
  * Interactive sliders for global list price adjustments (`-15%` to `+30%`), raw material/lumber inflation (`-20%` to `+40%`), and average trade discount tiers.
  * Automated price-elasticity volume modeling projecting 90-day net profit shifts instantly.

* **📄 Interactive CPQ & Quote-to-Order Desk:**
  * Configure custom client discount guardrails with built-in management approval thresholds.
  * One-click conversion from approved quotes straight into active workshop manufacturing orders.

* **📊 Pipeline & Kanban Board:**
  * 5-stage opportunity workflow with weighted probability calculations and instant stage-advancement actions.

* **🏢 Multi-Sector Dataset Engine (`⇄`):**
  * Seamlessly toggle between **Studio BI**, **Regency Urban Developments (Real Estate & Fit-Out)**, and **AdventureWorks Enterprise** datasets, or upload custom `.CSV` catalogues in-browser with zero data retention.

* **💻 6-Language Data Engineering Studio:**
  * Built-in interactive code sandbox showcasing production-grade **SQL (Window Functions & Pareto Analysis)**, **Power BI DAX**, **Python (Elasticity Forecasting)**, **C++20 (High-Frequency Pricing Engine)**, **Java Enterprise ETL**, and **ES6+ JavaScript**.

* **⌨️ Spotlight Command Palette (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`):**
  * Instant fuzzy-search across deals, accounts, and SKUs with 3 architectural ambiance themes (**Warm Architectural**, **Midnight Obsidian**, **Royal Emerald**).
